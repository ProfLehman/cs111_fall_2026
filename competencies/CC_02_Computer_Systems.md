## Competency Assessment

For CC-02, you should be prepared to:

- Identify major milestones and people in the history of computing
- Describe the progression from vacuum tubes to transistors to integrated circuits
- Explain how binary values can be represented by two-state digital hardware
- Create and interpret truth tables for NOT, AND, OR, and XOR gates
- Determine outputs for simple logic circuits
- Create a complete truth table for a simple circuit
- Explain the purpose of the CPU, CPU cores, GPU, RAM, cache, registers, SSDs, and hard drives
- Compare the relative speeds of registers, cache, RAM, and storage
- Explain clock frequency and the use of gigahertz (GHz)
- Recognize common types of computer systems
- Explain the basic idea of Moore's Law
- Interpret basic computer specifications such as processor, RAM, and storage

> NAND and NOR gates are not required for CC-02.
